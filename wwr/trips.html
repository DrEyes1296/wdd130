The Beehive Family Float

Perfect for families and first-timers! This half-day trip offers gentle rapids, beautiful scenery, and plenty of opportunities for swimming and fun. Enjoy a relaxing journey down a serene stretch of the river, guided by our friendly experts. Ages 5+.

Guardian's Glide

Our most popular full-day adventure! Experience a thrilling mix of Class II and III rapids that will get your heart pumping. This trip combines exciting whitewater with stunning canyon views and a delicious riverside lunch. No experience necessary, just an adventurous spirit! Ages 12+.

Weber Whitewater Whirl

For the adrenaline junkies! This is an intense, action-packed half-day trip through the challenging Class IV rapids of Weber Canyon. You'll paddle hard and navigate technical drops. Previous rafting experience is highly recommended. Ages 16+.
